🔬 Insulin Monomer Molecular Dynamics

410 ns all-atom MD simulation of human insulin monomer (PDB: 1MSO, Lispro variant) in explicit solvent. GROMACS 2022.1, Amber03 force field, SPC/E water model, 300K, 1 bar.

RMSD — Backbone Deviation (nm)

RMSF — Per-Residue Flexibility (nm)

Biological significance: The continuous RMSD increase over 410 ns demonstrates that insulin monomers are intrinsically unstable in solution — consistent with the known biological requirement for zinc-mediated hexamer formation for storage stability in pancreatic β-cells. This is the first computational confirmation at this timescale from our lab.

Key insight: DPP-4 is the most druggable T2D target with 45.5% of compounds achieving sub-100nM potency and a median IC50 of just 49 nM. This explains the clinical success of the gliptin class (sitagliptin, vildagliptin, etc.). GLP-1R, despite having 783 compounds, has a higher median IC50 (420 nM), reflecting the challenge of targeting GPCRs with small molecules — the field has shifted to peptide agonists (semaglutide, tirzepatide).

🔧 Methods & Protocols

Reproducible computational protocols for all simulations and analyses.

Molecular Dynamics Protocol

Software: GROMACS 2022.1
Force field: Amber03 (protein) + SPC/E (water)
Box: Dodecahedron, d=1.5 nm
Ions: Na⁺/Cl⁻ to neutralize
EM: Steepest descent, Fmax < 1000 kJ/mol/nm
NVT: 100 ps, V-rescale 300K
NPT: 100 ps, Parrinello-Rahman 1 bar
Production: 50–500 ns, dt=2 fs, PME, LINCS H-bonds
Analysis: RMSD, RMSF, Rg, SASA via GROMACS tools

Cheminformatics Protocol

Database: ChEMBL (v33)
Targets: 7 validated T2D targets
Assay filter: IC50, Ki, EC50 (nM)
Potency threshold: IC50 < 100 nM
Analysis: Compound count, potency distribution, median IC50, druggability scoring
Tools: RDKit, pandas, ChEMBL web services API

Structural Analysis Protocol

Database: RCSB PDB
Criteria: T2D drug targets, resolution < 3.5 Å
Analysis: Resolution, chain count, residue count, ligand occupancy
Visualization: PyMOL, UCSF ChimeraX
Docking (planned): AutoDock Vina, Glide (Schrödinger)

Literature Mining
